BANNER SPECIFICATIONS — Tomchei Shabbos of Florida

===========================================
1. ROSH HASHANAH CAMPAIGN PAGE — HERO HEADER
===========================================
Where it's used: The large image at the very top of the Rosh Hashanah donation
campaign page (behind the headline "A New Year. A Fresh Start. A Chance to
Make a Difference.").

Recommended size: 1920 x 700 px (landscape, roughly 2.7:1 aspect ratio)
Format: JPG or PNG

Notes:
- This image sits behind a dark overlay with white text on top of it, so it
  should work well when darkened/dimmed slightly.
- The section stretches the full width of the screen, so avoid important
  details (faces, text) near the very left/right edges, since it may be
  cropped differently on very wide or very narrow screens.
- Suggested theme: Rosh Hashanah / warm, hopeful imagery (apples, honey,
  pomegranates, shofar, etc.)

===========================================
2. POP-UP BANNER (site-wide announcement)
===========================================
Where it's used: A pop-up window that appears on the site after a few
seconds, announcing the Rosh Hashanah campaign, with a "Learn More & Donate"
button.

Recommended size: 1200 x 660 px (landscape, roughly 1.8:1 aspect ratio)
Format: JPG or PNG

Notes:
- This image fills the top portion of a popup card that is about 512px wide
  on screen — so submitting at 1200x660 gives enough resolution for it to
  look sharp (not blurry) on high-resolution/retina screens.
- A colored overlay (gold, ~70% opacity) sits on top of this image, with
  white text over it, so the image doesn't need to be dark — just needs to
  hold up okay with a tint over it.
- Same theme as above works well: Rosh Hashanah imagery, warm/inviting.
